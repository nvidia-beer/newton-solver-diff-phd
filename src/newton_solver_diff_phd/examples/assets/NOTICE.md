# Example assets and data

`poke_probe.usda` is a twelve-sector, 10 mm radius, 100 mm long probe with 26
vertices and 36 tetrahedra. These assets retain the Newton Developers'
Apache-2.0 attribution.

`cantilever.usda` is the original 756-node, 500-Hex8 grid generated by
`tools/bake_cantilever.py` from
[DiffPhD CantileverEnv3d at d319435](https://github.com/Akinesia112/DiffPhD/blob/d319435373a8a94aac2035bfe41f16f49bc1d2a1/python/py_diff_pd/env/cantilever_env_3d.py)
(MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 MIT Graphics Group). It preserves binary
000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111 corner ordering, cell/node indexing and the
20×5×5 grid at 0.016 m spacing. It stores a 900-triangle display surface,
root vertices and x-cell indices. No volume tetrahedra, material settings or
quadrature data are baked. Runtime Hex8 quadrature belongs to the solver.
The USD representation retains the Newton Developers' Apache-2.0 attribution.
Regenerate with `uv run --locked --extra examples python tools/bake_cantilever.py`.

`duck.usda` is the DiffPD duck: 5,130 vertices and 21,438 tetrahedra from
tetrahedralizing `asset/mesh/bob.obj` of
[diff_pd_public 1594aa0](https://github.com/mit-gfx/diff_pd_public/tree/1594aa07a6983ff9a9c107e9f0d83d07fa99c8d1)
(MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 MIT Graphics Group) with its `DuckEnv3d`
recipe, baked by `tools/bake_diff_pd_duck.py`. That `bob.obj` is a copy of
Keenan Crane's "Bob" from his
[3D model repository](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/ModelRepository/),
dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The UR5e model/meshes are copyright 2018 ROS Industrial Consortium, BSD-3-Clause;
see `universal_robots_ur5e/LICENSE` and the package third-party notices.

## Local DiffPhD bunny

`bunny_reference_local/` is generated by `tools/prepare_bunny_reference.py`
from a local DiffPhD checkout's `asset/mesh/bunny_watertight_simplified2.obj`.
It preserves the released tetrahedralization recipe, node and element ordering,
0.1 scale, geometric material regions and solid-angle contact vertex subset.
The manifest records source hashes, preparation versions and actual counts;
TetGen 0.8.4 produces 1,672 vertices and 5,950 tetrahedra for the checked source.
Mesher versions can change the generated mesh.

Credit: Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory. The
[Stanford 3D Scanning Repository terms](https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/)
allow research and free redistribution with acknowledgment; commercial use
requires permission. These mesh terms are separate from the solver's Apache-2.0
and DiffPhD code's MIT licenses. The generated assets remain local and are
excluded from Git, package distributions and Docker build contexts.

## Local PokeFlex recordings

`pokeflex_local/` contains authorized local recordings when installed by the
user. The prepared scene uses frames 18–53, a 13,781-vertex display surface,
affine embedding, and a 109-node/362-tetrahedron simulation mesh. Geometry is
in `die.usdc`, measured samples in `recording_debug.npz`, and their checksums in
`recording_debug.json`. Dataset preparation and access notices remain in the
private `provenance.json`.

The optional `recording_reference.json` selects the original DiffPhD preparation
recipe baked by `tools/prepare_poke_reference.py`: a 6,223-node/27,129-tetrahedron
die and a 1,165-node/4,389-tetrahedron probe in `die_reference.usdc` for the
recorded preparation versions. Counts can depend on mesher versions. Its
manifest records source hashes, preparation versions and coordinate transforms.
These are private derived assets subject to the same restrictions below.

PokeFlex: Jan Obrist, Miguel Zamora, Hehui Zheng, Ronan Hinchet, Firat Ozdemir,
Juan Zarate, Robert K. Katzschmann, and Stelian Coros.
[Dataset](https://pokeflex-dataset.github.io/) ·
[Access terms](https://pokeflex.ait.ethz.ch/).

**Recorded data and converted geometry must not be redistributed.** The private
subtree is ignored by Git and excluded from wheels, source archives and Docker
build contexts. Supply your authorized prepared recording with `--recording PATH`.
See [usage](../../../../docs/usage.md) for commands and required fields.
